### How I think about building things



**Start with the problem, not the tooling.** I've used FastAPI, Kubernetes, Celery, Azure, AWS, and plenty else. I pick what fits, not what's trending. The best infrastructure decision is often the boring one.

**Platform thinking over point solutions.** I'd rather build one system that serves ten use cases than ten bespoke services. Shared abstractions and a small surface area compound over time.

**Shipping is a skill.** Good architecture and best practices matter. But I've seen too many beautiful diagrams that never served a single user. I'd rather ship something simple that works than plan something perfect that doesn't.

**Design is not decoration.** I care about how things look and feel, not as a finishing touch, but as an engineering decision. Systems that are easy to understand are easier to trust, debug, and extend
