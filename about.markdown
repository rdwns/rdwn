### How I think about building things

**Start with the problem, not the tooling.** I've used FastAPI, Kubernetes, Celery, Azure, AWS, Databricks, and plenty else — but I pick what fits, not what's trending. The best infrastructure decision is often the boring one.

**Platform thinking over point solutions.** I'd rather build one system that serves ten use cases than ten bespoke services. Shared abstractions, clear boundaries, and a small surface area compound over time.

**Shipping is a skill.** Architecture diagrams don't serve users. I care about getting working software into production reliably, and I think the gap between "good engineer" and "great engineer" is mostly about the discipline to keep things simple enough to actually ship.

**Design is not decoration.** I bring visual and UX sensibility to technical work — not because it's a nice-to-have, but because systems that are easy to understand are easier to operate, debug, and extend. This site is a small example.
